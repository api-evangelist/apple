Apple will open its fifth Developer Center in Berlin later in 2026, joining existing locations in Cupertino, Shanghai, Bengaluru, and Singapore. The Berlin center will offer in-person sessions, workshops, labs, and one-on-one appointments with Apple experts in multiple languages.
